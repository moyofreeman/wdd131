Site Name:

www.halisupisecondary.ac.zw

Site Purpose:

This website serves as an information hub for Halisupi Secondary School. It provides learners, parents, prospective students and the general public with information about the school, including its location, demographics, curriculum, enrolment procedures, fee structure, academic calendar, school projects and employment opportunities.

Scenarios:

  • What are the school fees and other financial requirements?
  • What documents are required to enrol a new learner?
  • What subjects are offered at the school?
  • What main and extracurricular activities are available?
  • Where is the school located in relation to Gwanda Town?
  • What is the school's previous academic pass rate?
  • When does the school term begin and end?
  • Who is responsible for managing the school?
  • Is this a boarding school, day school or low-cost boarding school?
